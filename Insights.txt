📊 Insights from Netflix Data Analysis
1️⃣ Movies vs TV Shows

The Netflix catalog is heavily dominated by movies compared to TV shows.
This indicates that Netflix focuses strongly on quick-consumption content, as movies are faster to produce and license than TV series.

2️⃣ Top Content Producing Countries

The United States is the largest contributor to Netflix’s content library.
Countries like India and the United Kingdom are also emerging as major content producers, showing Netflix’s growing global expansion.

3️⃣ Netflix Content Growth Over Time

There is a significant surge in content addition after 2016, which marks Netflix’s global expansion phase.
In recent years, the growth has stabilized, indicating a more mature and established content strategy.

4️⃣ Content Rating Distribution

Most Netflix content falls under TV-MA and TV-14 ratings, meaning the platform primarily targets teen and adult audiences.
Content designed specifically for kids represents a smaller portion of the catalog.

5️⃣ Top Directors on Netflix

Several directors appear multiple times in the dataset, suggesting long-term partnerships between Netflix and specific creators.
This indicates Netflix’s strategy of building strong creator collaborations.

6️⃣ Most Popular Genres

The most common genres on Netflix are Drama, Comedy, and International Movies.
This shows Netflix prioritizes mass-appeal entertainment that can reach a global audience.

7️⃣ Movie Duration Distribution

Most movies fall within the 80–120 minute range, which aligns with the standard movie runtime.
Very long movies are relatively rare, suggesting Netflix prefers content that fits typical viewer attention spans.
